# Deposit Account Terms  (Policy Ref: POL-DEP-2024-02)

## Savings Accounts
- SB-GOLD-001 — premium savings: 3,000,000 minimum balance, 4.0% APY, unlimited free NEFT/RTGS.
- SB-REG-002 — regular savings: 10,000 minimum balance, 2.75% APY.
- SB-ZERO-003 — zero-balance salary account for corporate payroll tie-ups.

## Current Accounts
- CA-PREMIUM-004 — 25,000 monthly minimum; unlimited transactions; sweep-in enabled.

## Fixed Deposits
Tenures 7 days to 10 years. Rates: 1-year 5.00% APY, 3-year 4.85%, 5-year 4.60%. The
FD-TAX-SAVER product has a 5-year lock-in and qualifies under Section 80C.

## Payment Rails & Limits
- IMPS: up to 500,000 per transaction, 24x7.
- NEFT: no per-transaction limit; settled in half-hourly batches.
- RTGS: minimum 200,000 per transaction, for high-value transfers.

- Bandra (East) branch — IFSC HDFC0004567, MICR 400240045.
