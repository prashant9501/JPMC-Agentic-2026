# Retail Loan Products  (Policy Ref: POL-RTL-2024-07)

## Personal Loan
Unsecured. Eligibility: salaried applicants with 21-60 years age and minimum monthly
income 25,000; credit score 700+. Amount 50,000 to 2,000,000 over 12-60 months. Rate
10.5%-18% APR. Prepayment penalty: 4% of outstanding principal (nil after 24 months).

## Home Loan (Retail)
Secured against the property. Eligibility: age 21-65, stable income for 3+ years, credit
score 650+. Loan-to-value up to 80% (90% under first-time-buyer scheme). Rate from 8.4%.
Prepayment penalty: NIL for floating-rate home loans.

## Auto Loan
New vehicles from 8.9% APR, used vehicles up to 5 years old from 11.5% APR. Tenure up to
7 years. Requires 10% margin money.

## Education Loan
Covers tuition, living expenses, and equipment. Repayment begins 6 months after course
completion (moratorium period). Collateral required above 750,000.

## Gold Loan
Against gold ornaments at 75% of value. Rate 9.5%. Tenure up to 24 months. Product code
GL-RETAIL-004.
