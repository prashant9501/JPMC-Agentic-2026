# Credit Card Products  (Policy Ref: POL-CC-2024-05)

## Premium Rewards Card (SKU: CC-PREM-001)
Annual fee 95; APR 18.99%-25.99%; minimum score 720. Rewards: 3% travel, 2% dining, 1%
else. Sign-up bonus 500 after 3,000 spend in 90 days.

## Everyday Cash Card (SKU: CC-CASH-002)
No annual fee; APR 16.99%-23.99%; minimum score 670. 2% on groceries and fuel.

## Secured Card (SKU: CC-SEC-003)
For building credit. Security deposit 200-2,500 equals the limit. Graduates to unsecured
after 12 on-time payments.

## Transaction Decline Codes
- E-4021 — insufficient available credit / funds.
- E-5100 — card blocked or hotlisted (suspected fraud); customer must call the hotline.
- E-3300 — incorrect PIN entered three times; PIN locked for 24 hours.
- D-1200 — daily transaction limit exceeded; resets at midnight IST.

## General Terms
Grace period 25 days from statement close. Late fee up to 40. Balance transfer 0% for 15
months with a 3% fee. Foreign transaction fee 0% (Premium) or 3% (others).
