# Investment & Retirement Services  (Policy Ref: POL-INV-2024-09)

## Retirement Accounts
Traditional IRA 2024 contribution limit 7,000 (8,000 if age 50+). Roth IRA phases out
between 153,000 and 168,000 for single filers. SEP IRA allows 25% of compensation up to
69,000. National Pension System (NPS) tier-1 qualifies for an extra 50,000 deduction
under Section 80CCD(1B).

## Tax-Saving Investments
ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) mutual funds have a 3-year lock-in and qualify under
Section 80C (limit 150,000). PPF offers tax-free interest under the EEE regime.

## Certificates of Deposit
1-year 5.00% APY, 3-year 4.85%, 5-year 4.60%. Early withdrawal forfeits 3-12 months of
interest depending on tenure.

## Managed Portfolios
Conservative (70% debt), Balanced (50/50), Growth (70% equity). Quarterly rebalancing;
tax-loss harvesting above 100,000 in assets. Advisory fee 0.75%-1.25% per annum.
