# Commercial Lending Policy  (Policy Ref: POL-COM-2024-01)

## Term Loans
Eligibility: personal credit score 680+, business credit score (Paydex) 75+, 2+ years in
business, and 500,000 minimum annual revenue. Amounts 50,000 to 5,000,000 over 5-25 years.
Interest is Prime plus 2.5%-5.5% (currently 8.5%-11.5% APR). Origination fee 1-2%.

## Collateral (Clause 4.1)
Loan-to-value (LTV) ratios: real estate 75-80%, equipment 70-75%, inventory 50%. A
personal guarantee is required for loans above 250,000.

## Working Capital / Line of Credit
Revolving facility up to 1,000,000 at Prime plus 3%. Annual review required.

## SBA-style Guaranteed Loans
Government-guaranteed up to 85% of the loan for qualifying small businesses.
