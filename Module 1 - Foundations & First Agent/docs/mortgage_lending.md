# Mortgage Lending Policy  (Policy Ref: POL-MTG-2024-03)

## Conventional Mortgages
Minimum credit score 620; 740+ earns the best pricing. Down payment 20%, else
private mortgage insurance (PMI) is required. The maximum permissible ratio is 43%.
The 2024 conforming loan limit is 726,200. Rate: 6.5% APR (30-year fixed).

## FHA Loans
Down payment as low as 3.5% at a 580 score (or 10% down at 500). FHA charges an
upfront and an annual mortgage insurance premium (MIP). Rate: 6.25% APR.

## VA Loans
No down payment and no mortgage insurance for eligible veterans. A 2.3% funding fee
applies and may be financed. Rate: 6.0% APR.

## HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit)
Borrow against home equity at prime rate plus 1.5%. Draw period is 10 years, followed
by a 20-year repayment period. Minimum line 25,000; maximum 80% combined LTV.

## Prepayment (Clause 7.3.2)
Conventional and FHA loans have NO prepayment penalty. Fixed-rate jumbo loans carry a
2% penalty on the outstanding principal if closed within 3 years.
