# Tax Forms & Sections Reference  (Policy Ref: POL-TAX-2024-11)

## Forms
- Form 15G — declaration by a resident (below 60) to avoid TDS on interest when total
  income is below the taxable limit. Submit at the start of the financial year.
- Form 15H — the equivalent of Form 15G for senior citizens (60 and above).
- Form 16 — TDS certificate issued by an employer for salary income.
- Form 16A — TDS certificate for non-salary income (e.g., interest, commission).
- Form 26AS — consolidated annual tax statement showing all TDS credited against a PAN.

## Sections
- Section 80C — deduction up to 150,000 (ELSS, PPF, life insurance premium, 5-year FD).
- Section 80D — deduction for health insurance premium (25,000; 50,000 for seniors).
- Section 24(b) — deduction up to 200,000 on home-loan interest for a self-occupied property.
- Section 10(10D) — tax exemption on maturity proceeds of a life insurance policy.
- Section 80TTA — deduction up to 10,000 on savings-account interest.

## TDS on Interest
Banks deduct 10% TDS on interest above 40,000 per year (50,000 for seniors) unless a valid
Form 15G or Form 15H is on file.
