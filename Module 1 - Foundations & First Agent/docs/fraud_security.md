# Fraud Prevention & Account Security  (Policy Ref: POL-SEC-2024-06)

## Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all online banking and for any
transaction above 100. Biometric (fingerprint/face) login is supported on the mobile app.

## Card Controls
Freeze or unfreeze a card instantly, set per-channel limits, and block merchant categories
from the app. Report a lost or stolen card to the fraud hotline 1-800-BANK-911.

## Liability & Dispute
Customers have zero liability for unauthorized transactions reported within 60 days.
Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days (raise via SR-DISPUTE). Identity-theft
insurance covers up to 1,000,000 for premium account holders.

## Business Protection
Positive Pay matches presented cheques against an issued register. Dual authorization can
be enforced for transfers above a configurable threshold.
